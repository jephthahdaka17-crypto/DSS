WHO WE ARE

Building Innovative ICT Solutions for a Smarter Tomorrow

Daka Systems Solutions (DSS) is a forward-thinking Information and Communication Technology (ICT) company dedicated to helping businesses, institutions, and individuals embrace technology with confidence. We provide innovative, reliable, and cost-effective ICT solutions that improve productivity, strengthen security, and support sustainable growth.

Our expertise spans software development, networking, cybersecurity, CCTV and security systems, cloud computing, IT consultancy, and technical support. Every solution we deliver is tailored to meet our clients' unique needs while maintaining the highest standards of quality and professionalism.

Our Journey

Founded with a vision to bridge the gap between businesses and modern technology, Daka Systems Solutions continues to grow as a trusted ICT partner. Our journey is driven by innovation, continuous learning, and a commitment to delivering technology solutions that create lasting value for our clients and communities.
